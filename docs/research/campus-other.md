# other campus buildings — research notes (13 November 2026)
Status: draft

## Summary (≤ 10 lines: what the coding agent needs to know)
Do not model the Main Building here (that is R-1a / CD-1–CD-2). The buildings and grounds the CD-3–CD-5 agents need are real and mostly still standing: Larson Hall (a split-level hillside event building, also the likely home of Heron Books HQ), Dragon Hall (the only dormitory outside the Main Building, with the Student Lounge), a large beige gym north of the Main Building parking with a red  outdoor track and soccer infield just north of it, four lighted green-and-red tennis courts, an east-lawn basketball pad, a blue-and-yellow playground, an archery clearing, an equestrian barn-and-arena, a pond with a campground and a small shelter, a gravel bus lot, and two clusters of staff houses (“West Staff Housing” and “East Staff Housing” on the school’s own map). OpenStreetMap has only 16 unnamed building footprints; the gym, Larson Hall, most west staff houses, and a few others are in that extract, but east staff houses, barns, the track, courts, playground, pond shelter, bus lot, and any greenhouse are not. Game-world x,z is unknown (origin = centre of the game’s original satellite image, which was not in this research set); positions below are metres east and south of the Main Building footprint centroid 45.107650, −123.447950, from OSM and ESRI World Imagery. The hardest models are Larson Hall’s two-faced split-level massing, Dragon Hall plus the lounge, the many unmapped staff houses, the equestrian cluster, and anything the official tour never photographed (greenhouse / lath house, the RC car track itself, the pond gazebo close-up, and whether the Early Childhood Development Center was actually built).

## Facts

### How positions and sizes were measured
- What / where: All “east / south of the Main Building” figures use the centroid of OSM way 678519248 (`building=school`), 45.107650, −123.447950. East is positive X-like; south is positive Z-like, matching the game’s x-east, z-south convention in `AGENTS.md`. Game-world x,z: unknown.
- Size: OSM footprints were converted to metres with a local haversine (R = 6,378,137 m). Rectangular OSM ways are reported as side lengths of the footprint (not axis-aligned bounding boxes). ESRI World Imagery measurements use zoom-19 web-mercator tiles at 0.211 m/pixel at this latitude (tile 256 px ≈ 53.9 m). — confidence: medium-high for OSM rectangles that are clearly traced; medium for satellite measurements of painted or soft edges; low where a building is only identified by nearby context — source: [OpenStreetMap extract via Overpass, base date 27 October 2026](https://www.openstreetmap.org) (local copy `osm.json` / `osm2.json`); [ESRI World Imagery](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9) tiles at z19.
- Materials & colours: not applicable to the method — confidence: — — source: —
- Distinctive features: OSM tags none of the non-main buildings (all `building=yes` except the Main Building). The official illustrated campus map and 360° tour are the name source; satellite and OSM are the size/position source. `/workspace/school-game/DESIGN.md` and `art-tools/layout.py` name some of the same places but were used only as hints, never as sources.
- Photos: not applicable
- Notes for the game: do not drop OSM footprints onto campus as final models; `applyCampus` already replaces them. Prefer the named, measured items below. Credit OSM as ODbL when any footprint is used (see the R-3 licences file if it exists).

### Larson Hall / Heron Books HQ
- What / where: Larson Hall is the school’s event / gathering building, downhill and slightly east of the Main Building’s south front, on the slope that looks over the valley. Official tour tooltip: “Larson Hall Event Space.” Heron Books (the school’s curriculum publisher) has a separate tour stop, “Heron Books Headquarters,” with a signed brick entrance and office interiors; a further stop, “Heron Books Print Shop,” is a different house-like building. Approximate position of the OSM rectangle identified as Larson (way 678519247): 86 m east, 66 m south of the Main Building centroid, at about 45.107058, −123.446852. Game-world x,z: unknown.
- Size: OSM way 678519247 is a 4-vertex rectangle 31.8 m × 16.4 m (footprint about 521 m²). The downhill side reads as two storeys; the uphill entrance reads as one storey under a wide roof, so wall heights differ by side. Height: unknown (not in OSM). — confidence: medium-high that this OSM rectangle is the Larson footprint (it is the only sizable OSM building south-east of the Main Building, and official aerials show Larson in that foreground); medium for storey count; low for exact eave height — source: OSM way [678519247](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/678519247); [official aerial](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/f_auto,q_auto,t_image_size_4/v1733509476/delphian/pcpunwa0ykw5s135uuqu/AerialCampusShot.jpg); [Larson exterior, school tour](https://www.delphian.org/uploaded/tour/photos/hs_14_LarsonHall/LarsonHall_03.jpg).
- Materials & colours: Uphill/entrance side: blue-grey horizontal siding, a short buff-brick wainscot, white-framed double glass doors, building number “13” in gold/bronze figures, and a low-pitch standing-seam metal roof in grey-taupe that cantilevers out as a porch, held up by a slender white round column. Downhill/valley side: buff/tan brick on the lower storey with large white-framed windows and a recessed lower door; blue-grey siding and a continuous band of large square windows on the upper storey; the same metal roof with a deep overhang. A red-brick paver terrace runs along the valley side, with a modern cable/rod metal railing and two tall white lamp posts (square lantern heads). — confidence: high (multiple official exteriors) — source: [LarsonHall_03.jpg](https://www.delphian.org/uploaded/tour/photos/hs_14_LarsonHall/LarsonHall_03.jpg), [LarsonHall_04.jpg](https://www.delphian.org/uploaded/tour/photos/hs_14_LarsonHall/LarsonHall_04.jpg).
- Distinctive features: Split-level massing on a steep lawn; two very different public faces (siding + metal roof vs brick + ribbon windows); a long wall of glass on the event-space interior (white walls, cork/tan floor, used for events such as the Mothers’ Day Tea); Heron Books entrance is tan brick with a white “HERON BOOKS” sign over glass doors, a circular blue heron logo on the brick, a fabric pergola, and dark wicker patio chairs on a concrete pad. Official tour treats Larson Hall and Heron Books HQ as two named places; they may be two entrances or wings of one hillside complex, but that relationship is not stated in a primary source.

Game-world x,z: unknown.
- Size: If way 678519235 is this building, the footprint is 19.8 m × 33.9 m (about 672 m²). Official photos show three occupied storeys on the downhill/drive side, with a wooden deck at one end. Height: unknown. — confidence: medium for the OSM identification; high that the building is a 3-storey residential block with a downstairs or attached lounge; low for exact height — source: [Living at Delphian](https://www.delphian.org/start-here/student-life/living-at-delphian); OSM way [678519235](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/678519235); [DragonHallDorm_03.jpg](https://www.delphian.org/uploaded/tour/photos/hs_16_DragonHallDorm/DragonHallDorm_03.jpg).

### Gymnasium and track
- What / where: A large rectangular gymnasium just north of the Main Building parking, officially “Home of the Dragons,” with a red outdoor running track and grass soccer infield immediately north of the gym. Gym (OSM way 678519233) centroid about 45.109405, −123.447498, 35 m east and 195 m north of the Main Building (−195 m south). Track centre, from ESRI z19 imagery, about 45.11030, −123.44775, roughly 16 m east and 295 m north of the Main Building (about 100 m north of the gym). Game-world x,z: unknown.
- Size: Gym OSM footprint 28.7 m × 48.5 m (about 1,389 m²), long axis roughly north–south. Storeys: one tall volume (a gymnasium hall with an elevated indoor track). Height: unknown. Official gallery caption: “Faculty housing.” Game-world x,z: unknown.

Storeys: official “Faculty housing” photo and satellite shadows show a mix of 1- and 2-storey houses; at least one west-side building is a 2-storey grey-blue multi-unit with garage bays.
