# Project notes

The local working notes are deliberately private and excluded from Git:

- `PLAN.md`: current state, next action, open decisions and roadmap; keep below 15 KB.
- `CLAUDE.md`: controller rules, run commands, layout and live gotchas; keep below 10 KB.
- `docs/plan-history.md`: verbatim historical notes and completed decisions, read only when a task needs them.
- `.private-notes/`: local backup manifests and prepared note exports.

The public technical reference remains `AGENTS.md`. Do not load the history archive at startup.
Old instructions, approvals and unchecked historical lists do not establish current status or permission.
Before shortening either working note, preserve its complete original in the private archive and verify
that the archived bytes match. Retain uncertain or unresolved items in the current plan.
