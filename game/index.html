Hold W to spool the rotor up and lift off. Let go of the arrows and it levels itself and slows to a hover. To land, hold S over flat ground: it sinks gently near the ground. Coming down too hard or too fast, landing on a steep slope, or the rotor hitting the ground crashes it.

Your garage

Open Garage during free roam: choose car, bike and aircraft types (fighter jet, stealth fighter, prop plane, stunt plane or helicopter) with Type, or change paint, wheels, liveries, race numbers and rider colors. Drag the preview to turn it; scroll to zoom. Changes are saved in this browser.

Save up to three looks for each vehicle and load them whenever you like. Wash & repair clears dust, wetness and scuffs. Cosmetic changes do not affect performance.

Light & weather

Every game starts in the morning; a full day lasts 50 minutes (morning 5, day 20, dusk 5, night 20), and showers come and go. Vehicle lights switch on at dusk. In Settings → Time of day & weather jump to morning, day, dusk or night, choose random, clear or rainy weather, or turn the cycle off and keep one look: clear midday, golden hour, misty morning, rose dusk, moonlit night or valley rain. Roads get slippery in the rain and stay wet briefly after a shower. Allow more room to turn and brake.

Map & navigation

Choose a starting point each time you open the game; H returns there. Zoom the flat map from the whole valley down to individual streets and buildings. More detail appears as you move closer.
